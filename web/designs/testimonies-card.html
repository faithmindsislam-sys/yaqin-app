The two testimonies of faith

To become Muslim, a person declares the two testimonies of faith, sincerely believing in their meaning:

✦

First

أشهد أن لا إله إلا الله،

I bear witness that there is no deity worthy of worship except Allah,

✦

Second

وأشهد أن محمدًا رسول الله

and I bear witness that Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah.
