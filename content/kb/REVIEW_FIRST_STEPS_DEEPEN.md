# Reviewer checklist: First Steps and Deepen drafts

Every course below is a `draft` and every new source is `pending`. A qualified reviewer
ticks each item or corrects the course, approves its sources, then approves the course.
Items marked **[C]** are matters of scholarly difference: the course must present the
difference without choosing a side. "Needs a source" lists statements a reviewer should
either back with an approved source or remove.

Drafted with AI assistance from stored, verbatim sources, then audited against those sources
by a second pass. See [`docs/CURRICULUM_FIRST_STEPS_DEEPEN.md`](../../docs/CURRICULUM_FIRST_STEPS_DEEPEN.md).
